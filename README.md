# Board-Exam-PYQs-And-Sample-Papers.

A centralized collection of **CBSE Board Examination Previous Year Question Papers (PYQs) and Sample Question Papers**, designed to help students prepare smarter, practise effectively, and perform better in their board examinations.

Whether you're revising a particular subject, attempting full-length papers, or familiarising yourself with the board examination pattern, this repository aims to make quality study resources easier to access.

---

## 🎯 What's Inside?

This repository focuses on providing academic resources for CBSE Class 10 students.

| Subject | Resources |

| Mathematics (Standard) | Previous Year Papers & Sample Papers |
| Science | Previous Year Papers & Sample Papers |
| Social Science | Previous Year Papers & Sample Papers |

| Malayalam | Previous Year Papers & Sample Papers |

### 📄 Types of Resources

- **Previous Year Question Papers (PYQs):** Actual papers from previous board examinations.
- **Sample Question Papers:** Practice papers to understand examination patterns.
- **Additional Practice Resources:** Other relevant examination preparation materials as they are added.

## 💡 How to Use These Resources Effectively

1. **Understand the syllabus:** Make sure you know which chapters are included in your examination.
2. **Practise chapter-wise:** Strengthen individual topics before attempting complete papers.
3. **Attempt papers under timed conditions:** Simulate the actual examination environment.
4. **Analyse your mistakes:** Identify weak areas and revise them.
5. **Repeat:** Consistent practice is more effective than last-minute preparation.

## 🤝 Contributions

Contributions are welcome!

If you have additional PYQs, sample papers, answer keys, or other useful CBSE study materials, feel free to contribute.

### How to Contribute

1. Fork this repository.
2. Create a new branch for your changes.
3. Add the relevant resources in the appropriate subject folder.
4. Ensure files are correctly named and organised.
5. Submit a Pull Request describing your contribution.

Please ensure that:
- Uploaded documents are relevant to CBSE examinations.
- Files are readable and correctly labelled.
- The examination year and subject are clearly identifiable.
- Duplicate files are avoided.
